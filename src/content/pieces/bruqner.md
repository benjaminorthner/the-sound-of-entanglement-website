*BruQner* was written for the Mariendom in Linz, one of the few churches in
Europe with two organs that are tuned to each other and can be played
together. The musical material comes from Anton Bruckner's *Präludium in C*,
the "Perger Präludium" (WAB 129, 1884). It was performed twice: the premiere
on 4 September 2024, in English, and a family-friendly afternoon performance
in German on 6 September.
