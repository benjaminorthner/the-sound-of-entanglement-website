*BruQner* was written for the Mariendom in Linz, one of the few churches in
Europe with two organs that are tuned to each other and can be played
together. It premiered on 4 September 2024, Anton Bruckner's 200th birthday,
as the opening of the Ars Electronica Festival.

The musical material comes from Bruckner's *Präludium in C* (the "Perger
Präludium", WAB 129, 1884). Clemens Wenger turned it into more than 250
precomposed bars: some identical to Bruckner's, many varied, inverted,
transposed or rhythmically intensified. For every photon pair, the experiment
picks one of four motifs for each organ, labelled by the setting that was
measured and its outcome: A1+, A1−, A2+, A2− for Alice, and the same for Bob.
The motifs are arranged so that the stronger-than-classical correlations
mostly produce polyrhythms.

Gerhard Raab played Alice on the Rudigier organ at the back of the nave.
Wolfgang Kreuzhuber played Bob on the choir organ at the front. They sat about
thirty metres apart and read their next bar from a tablet, one bar ahead, over
cabled network: WiFi was too unreliable for music.

## Form

Seven short sections. The first three are conducted by entangled photons. In
the fourth, the experiment switches between entangled photons and a classical
setting, and the correlations become weaker (the Bell value falls below 2). In
the fifth, the organists improvise freely, "a bow to human free will and to
Anton Bruckner, who was known as one of the greatest improvisers on the
organ". The last two return to the quantum world and end with the *Perger
Präludium* in an extended version by Franz Neuhofer.

## Light

The photons in the experiment are infrared and invisible, so the visuals draw
where they come from. In the crystal, entangled pairs leave along two cones of
light. Enar de Dios Rodríguez and Benjamin Orthner turned that into projected
rings: three laser projectors faced the audience and shone into haze, so each
ring became a cone of light in the air above them. Circles meant entanglement,
straight lines meant classical correlations, and every shape was triggered by
a measured photon pair.
