Viva Quantum, an invitation-only conference for the International Year of
Quantum Science and Technology, met at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum
Optics in Garching and at the Deutsches Museum in Munich. The talk presented
BruQner, 8 Rooms and Indeterminate Apparatus.
