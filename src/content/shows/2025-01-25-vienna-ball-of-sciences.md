*8 Rooms* premiered at the Vienna Ball of Sciences, in six short sets
between 21:30 and after two in the morning. There was no lecture: guests
came and went, danced to the clicks of the wave plates, and asked
questions at the case between sets. Over the night the experiment
measured a Bell value of 2.62.
