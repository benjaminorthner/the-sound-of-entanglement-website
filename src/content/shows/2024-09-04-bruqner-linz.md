The premiere took place on the evening of 4 September 2024, the 200th
birthday of Anton Bruckner, as the opening event of the Ars Electronica
Festival and part of the Upper Austrian KulturEXPO *antonbruckner2024*.
More than 3 000 people came to the Mariendom. Admission was free.

The experiment stood at the crossing, in a glass case lit from inside.
The two organists sat at the two organs, about thirty metres apart,
reading motifs chosen for them by the photon pairs. Three laser
projectors faced the audience and shone into haze, so the rings in the
visuals became cones of light in the air. In the middle of the piece the
experiment was switched to classical correlations, and the Bell value
fell below 2.

A second, family-friendly performance with a German lecture followed on
the afternoon of 6 September.
