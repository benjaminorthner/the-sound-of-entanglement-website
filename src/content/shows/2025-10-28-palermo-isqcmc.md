> Quantum entanglement gives rise to correlations that no classical mechanism
> can replicate. We present an experimental-artistic platform that converts
> the joint measurement outcomes of separated entangled photons into
> real-time musical and visual material, enabling two or more musicians to
> perform structures that breach classical limits. A compact photonic
> Bell-test experiment streams real-time data to a low-latency mapping layer
> written in Max8 and TouchDesigner, which translates each outcome pair into
> synchronised performance cues and generative graphics. By electronically
> rotating the measurement bases, we dynamically vary the strength of the
> quantum correlations; this drives multiple musical parameters, allowing
> audiences to hear the statistical gap between classically correlated and
> quantum-correlated music.

The experiment stayed in Vienna: the demonstration used a simulated stream
with the same statistics.
