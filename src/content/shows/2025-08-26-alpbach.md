The first performance as a trio, at the European Forum Alpbach. At the
sides of the stage the audience could follow the Bell value as it was
measured, with a line marking the classical limit of 2. After the
performance the case was opened and the audience came on stage to look
inside.

Erwin Schrödinger spent his last years in Alpbach and is buried there.
