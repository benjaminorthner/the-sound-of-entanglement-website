Clemens Wenger is a composer and performer for stage and media, and
professor of composition at the Anton Bruckner Private University in Linz,
specialising in jazz and eclectic music. He founded the JazzWerkstatt Wien
and plays keys in the band 5/8erl in Ehr'n. For *The Sound of
Entanglement* he writes the music and builds the mappings that turn
measurements into scores, sounds and cues.
