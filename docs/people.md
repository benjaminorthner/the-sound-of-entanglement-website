**Author contributions** (paper, verbatim): "P.H., J.K. and C.W. conceived the
concept of The Sound of Entanglement with the help of E.d.D.R., R.K., and M.R..
B.O., M.R. and P.H. designed the experimental Bell setup. B.O. built the setup
under the supervision of P.H., with input from all authors, and developed the
live communication/transfer of data between the quantum experiment and the
musical/visual controls. C.W. developed the artistic concept and designed the
live performances, with input from all authors. E.d.D.R. created the artistic
concept of the live visual elements, with input from all authors. A.P.
coordinated the preparation and completion of the manuscript, to which all
authors contributed, and produced the documentary."

## Short bios (for a team page)

Draft bios from the Leonardo manuscript (Sept 2025), edited lightly. Get
approval from each person before publishing.

- **Clemens Wenger** is a composer and performer for stage and media, and
  professor of composition at Anton Bruckner University in Linz, specialising in
  jazz and eclectic music. (Longer: born 1982 in Krems, based in Vienna, founder
  of JazzWerkstatt Wien, keys in 5/8erl in Ehr'n, *Physics of Beauty*.)
- **Benjamin Orthner** is an experimental quantum physicist at TU Wien who
  brings together his scientific work with a personal passion for visual and
  musical art. (Alpbach bio: "turns photons into live music: coding, composing
  and animating data so audiences see and hear entanglement".) *The paper's
  version still says "master's student"; confirm his current status.*
- **Philipp Haslinger** is a quantum physicist at TU Wien, specialising in
  quantum-enhanced sensing and imaging. (START Prize 2018; co-founder of SEEC
  Photography with Enar de Dios Rodríguez.)
- **Johannes Kofler** researches and lectures in quantum information theory at
  JKU Linz, at the interface between quantum physics and computer science.
- **Richard Küng** is full professor and head of the Department of Quantum
  Information and Computation at Kepler (QUICK), JKU Linz, working between
  computer science, physics and applied mathematics.
- **Martin Ringbauer** is Assistant Professor at the University of Innsbruck,
  working on quantum computing and quantum simulation with trapped ions.
- **Alexander Ploier** is at the Department of Quantum Information and
  Computation at Kepler (QUICK), JKU Linz, and works as a filmmaker at the
  intersection of arts and science.
- **Enar de Dios Rodríguez** is a visual artist whose research-based projects
  reflect on the production of space and its sociopolitical and environmental
  implications. (Born 1986 in Spain, based in Vienna. MFA from the San Francisco
  Art Institute. Marianne.von.Willemer-Preis 2024.)
- **Judith Schwarz**: drummer and composer based in Vienna (b. 1989), with a
  focus on improvised music and jazz. https://www.judithschwarz.at/
- **Manu Mayr**: Vienna-born bassist, composer and producer. Teaches double
  bass at mdw. Member of schtum, PRCDR, 5K HD and Kompost 3. https://manumayr.com/
