# The project

*The Sound of Entanglement* (TSOE) is an art–science project in which a live
Bell-test experiment with entangled photons conducts music and visuals in real
time. It started as Benjamin Orthner's master thesis at TU Wien and became a
touring lecture-performance.

## One-paragraph version

A violet laser (405 nm) shines through a nonlinear crystal. Very rarely, about
one photon in 10¹¹ splits into a pair of infrared (810 nm) photons that are
entangled in polarisation. The two photons go to two measurement stations,
**Alice** and **Bob**. About twice a second, motorised half-wave plates on each
side pick one of two measurement settings at random, and each photon lands in
one of two detectors (+1 or −1). Each outcome is random on its own, but the
correlations between Alice and Bob are stronger than any classical system can
produce: the Bell value *S* reaches ≈ 2.4–2.6 on stage, where no classical
conductor can exceed 2. These outcomes are sent over the network (OSC) to the
composer's Max/MSP patch and to the visuals, and they decide what the musicians
play. The experiment is the **quantum conductor**.

## Key ideas (use this vocabulary)

- **Quantum conductor.** The Bell setup takes the role of the conductor or VJ.
  "No human conductor or classical computer could instruct such music — only
  the strange physics of quantum particles can do that."
- **Quantum aleatoric music.** It extends chance music (Ockeghem, the Mozart and
  Haydn dice games, Cage, Xenakis, Riley's *In C*) with *objective*
  randomness and *non-classical correlations*. All earlier chance music used
  "deterministic randomness", random only because of our ignorance.
- **Audible quantum vs classical.** Every piece has a classical section: the
  wave plates are turned to angles where S ≤ 2. The clicks stay random, but the
  correlations change, and the music is composed so that the difference can be
  heard.
- **Unique and unrepeatable.** "Over the total performance time of
  approximately 20 minutes we get more options than there are atoms in our
  universe."
- **Not a simulation.** "We are not working with data collections, computer
  simulations or metaphorical analogies to quantum physics — we have built a
  new musical instrument… We measure their properties in real time and look at
  nature as it is here and now." (lecture script)

## Format

A **lecture-performance**: about 20–25 minutes of accessible physics lecture
(often by Johannes Kofler, Philipp Haslinger, Richard Küng, Martin Ringbauer or
Benjamin), then about 20 minutes of live audiovisual performance, often followed
by Q&A. At the Vienna Ball of Sciences it ran as six 15-minute sets without a
lecture. The experiment sits on stage in a lit glass case.

The lecture's closing line: *"You will have a lifetime left to think about
quantum physics and its consequences, but now only 20 minutes to experience
entanglement in a live performance."*

## History (short)

- **2022**: The idea forms around a seminar in Traunkirchen (Wenger, Haslinger,
  Küng, Kofler). The application to the Upper Austrian **Brucknerjahr 2024
  ProjektCALL** went in in Nov 2022, with working title "BruQner – von der
  klassischen Musik zur Quantenphysik". The first concept used quantum
  computers. (The OÖ Landespreis documents say "summer 2023"; that is
  contradicted by the 2022 application. See `open-questions.md`.)
- **May–June 2023**: The concept switches from quantum computers to a live Bell
  test with entangled photons. Benjamin joins to build the experiment.
- **4 & 6 Sept 2024**: premiere of *BruQner* in the Mariendom Linz, opening Ars
  Electronica 2024 on Bruckner's 200th birthday, to 3 000+ people. The team
  "worked for over 16 months to make it happen".
- **2025**: Tour with *8 Rooms* (band): Vienna Ball of Sciences, Johns Hopkins
  University in Washington DC, Tech Forum Millstatt, European Forum Alpbach.
  Also an EXPO 2025 Osaka presence (a video in the Austrian Pavilion).
  *Indeterminate Apparatus* premieres at CIVA (Belvedere 21, Vienna) and plays
  again at JKU. arXiv paper (Sept 2025). Clemens Wenger becomes Professor of
  Composition at Bruckner University Linz (Oct 2025).
- **2026**: Pint of Science talk (May). Album *Sounds of Entanglement* by the
  band **The Uncertain Question** is in production: recording planned for
  1 May 2026, release planned for Oct/Nov 2026 (status unconfirmed). Next show:
  **16 Oct 2026, University of Innsbruck**. Wien Modern (Nov 2026) is
  tentative.

## Names and spelling

- Project: **The Sound of Entanglement**. Not "Sounds of…"; that is the album
  title. Abbreviated TSOE internally.
- First piece: **BruQner** (capital Q), often written "BruQner – The Sound of
  Entanglement".
- Live band: **The Uncertain Question** (TUQ; OSC addresses use `/TUQ/…`).
- German: "Der Klang der Verschränkung".
