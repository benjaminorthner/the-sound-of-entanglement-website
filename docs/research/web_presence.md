## 0. Key facts to cite (with sources)

- **Premiere:** "BruQner – The Sound of Entanglement", 4 Sep 2024, 22:00 (English), repeated 6 Sep 2024, 16:00 (German). Mariendom / New Cathedral Linz. It opened the Ars Electronica Festival 2024 and was part of antonbruckner2024 (OÖ KulturEXPO), on Bruckner's 200th birthday. Admission was free. Sources: quantumscience.at/bruqner-sound, anton-bruckner-2024.at, TU Wien.
- **Organists at the premiere:** Wolfgang Kreuzhuber and Gerhard Raab. One photon of each pair was sent to the side organ and its partner to the main organ, and both organists varied Bruckner's *Perger Präludium* (WAB 129). Source: uibk newsroom.
- **Audience:** "over 3,000" (austria.org, Alpbach) or "3,100" (Bruckner University news).
- **Premiere funding and support:** a project of OÖ KulturEXPO Anton Bruckner 2024 in cooperation with Ars Electronica. Supported by LIT-JKU, VCQ, Universität Innsbruck, TU Wien, SFB BeyondC and Stadt Wien Kultur. The later touring was supported by JKU, Uni Innsbruck, TU Wien and the FWF Cluster of Excellence quantA. The paper also lists BMWKMS (Staatsstipendium Komposition).
- **Development time:** the documentary says the team "worked for over 16 months to make it happen".
- **Second piece, "8 Rooms"** (Clemens Wenger, Manu Mayr, Judith Schwarz, registered with AKM in 2025). Premiered 25 Jan 2025 at the Vienna Ball of Sciences (City Hall) and repeated 14 Apr 2025 at the JHU Science Diplomacy Summit. It uses "(i) digital audio processing of live sound that is reactive to the measurements and (ii) a 2-dimensional quantum random walk on a periodic square grid that controls the musical form" (arXiv paper).
- **BruQner registration:** "Clemens Wenger and Anton Bruckner, 'BruQner,' akm, ISWC: 3307761800 (2024)". This is reference [26] in arXiv:2509.08892.
- **Live band name:** "The Uncertain Question" (Judith Schwarz, drums; Manu Mayr, electric bass; Clemens Wenger, keys). Source: current soundofentanglement.com under-construction page.
- **Author contributions (from the paper):** "P.H., J.K. and C.W. conceived the concept … B.O., M.R. and P.H. designed the experimental Bell setup. B.O. built the setup under the supervision of P.H. … and developed the live communication/transfer of data between the quantum experiment and the musical/visual controls. C.W. developed the artistic concept and designed the live performances … E.d.D.R. created the artistic concept of the live visual elements … A.P. coordinated the preparation and completion of the manuscript … and produced the documentary."
- **Project lead:** the 2024 materials name Philipp Haslinger (TU Wien) as "Projektleitung".

## 5. Team: official profiles and short bios

- **Clemens Wenger:** composer and musician (contemporary jazz, pop, electronic), born 1982 in Krems and based in Vienna. Univ.-Prof. of Composition at Anton Bruckner Privatuniversität Linz since Oct 2025. He founded JazzWerkstatt Wien and plays keys in 5/8erl in Ehr'n. His *Physics of Beauty* won the Content Vienna main prize. Profile: https://www.bruckneruni.ac.at/de/personen/clemens-wenger-2 · Alpbach: https://www.alpbach.org/speakers/wenger-clemens
- **Enar de Dios Rodríguez:** visual artist (born 1986 in Spain, based in Vienna). She works in video, photography and installation from feminist, posthumanist and decolonial perspectives. She was an ifk Junior Fellow (Kunstuniversität Linz), is a member of The Golden Pixel Cooperative, and received the City of Vienna Prize for Media Art. Site: https://enardediosrodriguez.com/about/
- **Martin Ringbauer:** Assistant Professor at the Institute for Experimental Physics, Universität Innsbruck. He leads a quantum computing group (qudit quantum technology), holds an ERC Starting Grant, won the Hans und Walter Thirring Prize, and is an avid science communicator. Group: https://www.uibk.ac.at/en/sp-physik/forschung/forschungsgruppen/experimental-physics/qudit-quantum-technology/ · Alpbach: https://www.alpbach.org/speakers/ringbauer-martin
- **Johannes Kofler:** Senior Lecturer in quantum information and foundations at the Institute for Integrated Circuits and Quantum Computing, JKU Linz. "Researches and lectures in quantum information theory at the interface between quantum physics and computer science." Site: https://johanneskofler.com/ · Alpbach: https://www.alpbach.org/speakers/kofler-johannes
- **Richard Küng:** Univ.-Prof. and head of the Department of Quantum Information and Computation at Kepler (QUICK), JKU Linz. Formerly at Caltech (IQIM). He holds an ERC Starting Grant and is a member of the Young Academy of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (ÖAW). Profile: https://www.jku.at/en/institute-for-integrated-circuits-and-quantum-computing/about-us/team/univ-prof-dr-richard-kueng/
- **Alexander Ploier:** research assistant at the Institute for Integrated Circuits and Quantum Computing, JKU Linz. His focus is science–art and making research accessible, and he produced the documentary. Profiles: https://www.jku.at/en/institute-for-integrated-circuits-and-quantum-computing/about-us/team/alexander-ploier/ · https://research.jku.at/en/persons/alexander-ploier
- **Benjamin Orthner:** experimental quantum physicist (MSc) at the Atominstitut, TU Wien, and lead developer of the experiment and software integration. - Abstract (verbatim): "The advent of quantum physics has revolutionized our understanding of the universe, replacing the deterministic framework of classical physics with a paradigm dominated by intrinsic randomness and quantum correlations. This shift has not only enabled groundbreaking technologies, such as quantum sensors, networks and computers, but has also unlocked entirely new possibilities for artistic expressions. In this paper, we explore the intersection of quantum mechanics and art, focusing on the use of quantum entanglement and inherent randomness as creative tools. Specifically, we present The Sound of Entanglement, a live musical performance driven by real-time measurements of entangled photons in a Bell test. By integrating the measured quantum correlations as a central compositional element and synchronizing live visuals with experimental data, the performance offers a unique and unrepeatable audiovisual experience that relies on quantum correlations which cannot be produced by any classical device.
