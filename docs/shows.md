## Resolved discrepancies

- **BruQner dates: 4 & 6 Sept 2024**, not 4 & 5. The bruQner repo's
  `docs/performance_history.md` is wrong about this.
- **8 Rooms premiere: 25 Jan 2025.** Sets ran past midnight, hence "26 Jan" in
  some notes.
- **CIVA: 3 Oct 2025**; 2 Oct was setup.
- **Zirkus des Wissens: 19 Nov 2025**; a quantA page says 21 Nov, which is wrong.
- **Audience:** say "over 3 000" (some sources say 3 100 or 3 200).
- **Osaka** was a video exhibition, not a concert. The "Osaka" photo folder is
  actually 2024 pre-premiere sessions.
